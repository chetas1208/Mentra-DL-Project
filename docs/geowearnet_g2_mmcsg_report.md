## ERROR ANALYSIS (WS66)

- trigger: recording 1302664060426140_0001_3375_22000 scored worse-than-chance zero-shot AUROC on ALL 7 channels simultaneously in zero_shot_channel_matrix_train_val.json

- verdict: **ISOLATED_LABEL_ANOMALY**

- flagged recording: `1302664060426140_0001_3375_22000` self/other RMS ratio=0.214 vs population median=1.944 (n_recordings_below_0.5_ratio=1 of 172)

- Exactly 1 of 172 recordings has a self/other RMS ratio below 0.5 (1302664060426140_0001_3375_22000, ratio=0.214), more than 3x lower than the next-lowest recording, against a population median ratio of 1.94. This uniform, severe, single-recording inversion (worse-than-chance AUROC on every one of 7 independent microphones) is the signature of a SPEAKER-ROLE LABEL SWAP in that recording's official RTTM, not a genuine acoustic hard case or a GeoWearNet model failure. Per campaign rules, the raw MMCSG RTTM is NOT modified; this is recorded as a known, isolated (1/172) label-quality defect. It is not excluded from already-computed aggregate metrics (which are frame-count-weighted over many recordings, so one mislabeled ~3-minute recording has a small, diluted effect on pooled AUROC) but should be kept in mind if this specific recording ever dominates a small-n analysis (e.g. a single-recording qualitative demo).



## MAJOR NEGATIVE RESULTS (preserved, not hidden)

- Real E0 physical heuristic is loudness-dominated on MMCSG (MMCSG_E0_LOUDNESS_ONLY), a WEAKER spectral-survives-amplitude-removal story than simulation showed (G1 spectral-only ~0.946 AUROC vs real level-matched ~0.63 on channel 2).
- Channels 5/6 (whichever physical mic positions those are) transfer much worse zero-shot (~0.74-0.77 AUROC) than channels 0-4 (~0.91-0.97) -- transfer is channel-position-dependent.
- Naive 7-mic averaging is WORSE than the best single channel (phase cancellation), a reminder that "more channels" is not automatically better without real spatial processing.
- Identity/session information leaks substantially into the G2 embedding (IDENTITY_SHORTCUT_SUSPECTED) -- flagged, not swept under the rug.
- No official Meta beamformer/RIR/MCAC resources were available locally, so the true mouth-beam upper bound could not be reproduced (only a cheap spatial-feature proxy).

## BLOCKERS SOLVED

- sklearn API drift (`LogisticRegression(multi_class=...)` removed in sklearn 1.9) fixed in `identity_probe.py`.
- G1's context-variant checkpoints were trained with `arch=tcn`, not the CRNN chosen as G1's overall winner -- the WS34 context sweep was re-targeted to fine-tune each context's OWN matching G1 TCN checkpoint instead of silently loading mismatched weights.
- Heavy multi-tenant CPU contention on the shared host (other users' jobs) slowed several CPU-bound census/probe scripts; solved by running them at low `nice` priority rather than blocking GPU training.
